❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is this collage maker completely free?
Yes — 100% free with no restrictions. No account, no watermarks, no daily limits and no hidden charges. All layouts, all quality levels and all download formats are available to everyone permanently.
Are my photos uploaded to a server?
Never. All processing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photos are read into browser memory and combined on a canvas element — zero network activity occurs. Your personal photos never leave your device.
What image formats can I upload?
You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP and most standard image formats. Your browser's native Image API handles decoding, so virtually all common formats are supported including photos taken directly from your phone camera.
What resolution will the downloaded collage be?
The collage canvas renders at 2× pixel density for sharp, high-resolution output. Download quality can be set to Web (70%), High (92%) or Max (100%). PNG downloads are always lossless regardless of quality setting.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes. The tool is fully mobile-responsive and works on Android and iOS in Chrome and Safari. Upload photos from your gallery, create the collage and download directly to your device — no app installation needed.
